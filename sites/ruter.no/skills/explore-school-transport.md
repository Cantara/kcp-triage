# Skill: Explore School Transport (Skoleskyss) on Ruter.no

## Overview
Ruter.no provides information and portals for **skoleskyss** (school transport) — a subsidized service for eligible students in Oslo and Akershus. This skill covers navigating school transport information, eligibility, and how to apply.

## Accessing School Transport Information

### Method 1: From Navigation
1. Click **"Skoleskyss"** in the top navigation bar
2. You are taken to https://ruter.no/skoleskyss

### Method 2: From Homepage
1. Visit https://ruter.no/
2. Look for the **"Skoleskyss"** section in the main menu

### Method 3: Direct Access
1. Visit https://ruter.no/skoleskyss

## Understanding the Two Regions

### Important: Oslo vs. Akershus
School transport is managed by **different organizations** depending on where the student lives:

**1. Akershus (Ruter manages)**
- Ruter AS handles school transport applications and logistics
- Covers all municipalities in Akershus (Asker, Lilehammer, Lillehammer, etc.)
- Eligibility criteria set by Akershus fylkeskommune (county)
- Apply via dedicated Akershus portal

**2. Oslo (Oslo Kommune manages)**
- Oslo municipality (Oslo kommune) handles school transport
- Ruter is NOT the primary provider; links to Oslo kommune resources
- Click link "Se Oslo kommunes nettsider" to access
- URL: https://www.oslo.kommune.no/skole-og-utdanning/skoleskyss-og-skolevei/

## Skoleskyss i Akershus

### Accessing Akershus School Transport
1. Visit https://ruter.no/skoleskyss/skoleskyss-i-akershus
2. You will see:
   - Overview of skoleskyss program
   - Eligibility criteria
   - How to apply
   - Link to **application portal**

### Eligibility in Akershus
Students may qualify if:
- Living in Akershus and attending a **mandatory school** (grades 1-10, ages ~6-16)
- OR attending **upper secondary school** (videregående skole, ages 16-19) — different rules apply
- Distance from home to school exceeds a threshold (typically 3-4 km for younger students, longer for secondary)
- No safe walking/cycling route available
- Economic/family circumstances (means-tested)

**Important note:** Eligibility is determined by **Akershus fylkeskommune**, not Ruter. Ruter provides the service but does not set eligibility rules.

### How to Apply (Akershus)
1. Ruter operates a new **skoleskyss-løsning** (school transport solution) — replacing the old "RuterBestilling" system
2. Access the **dedicated portal** via link on the skoleskyss page
3. You will need:
   - Student's personal ID (Norwegian personnummer)
   - Parent/guardian contact info
   - Home address and school address
   - Reason for application (distance, safety, accessibility)
4. Submit application
5. Akershus fylkeskommune reviews and makes decision
6. If approved, student receives transport pass/ticket

### Information About Videregående (Upper Secondary) Students
The skoleskyss page mentions:
- "Er du elev ved videregående skole i Akershus?" (Are you a student at secondary school in Akershus?)
- Special rules and application process for students age 16+
- May have different eligibility or cost-sharing requirements
- See dedicated section on page for details

## Skoleskyss i Oslo

### Accessing Oslo School Transport
1. Visit https://ruter.no/skoleskyss (main skoleskyss page)
2. Find the **"Skoleskyss i Oslo"** section
3. Click **"Se Oslo kommunes nettsider"** (See Oslo kommune's website)
4. You are taken to https://www.oslo.kommune.no/skole-og-utdanning/skoleskyss-og-skolevei/

### Why Oslo is Separate
- Oslo kommune (municipality) operates its own school transport system
- Different eligibility rules and application process than Akershus
- Ruter does not manage Oslo skoleskyss
- All Oslo-specific information on oslo.kommune.no

## New Skoleskyss Solution (2024+)

### What Changed?
Ruter announced:
- **Old system:** "RuterBestilling" (legacy portal)
- **New system:** "ny skoleskyss-løsning" (new school transport solution)
- Transition began in 2024

### For Users
- Old RuterBestilling portal is being **phased out**
- Students/parents must use the **new portal** for applications
- Link to new portal on https://ruter.no/skoleskyss/nyhet
- More details on the "Ny skoleskyss-løsning tar over for RuterBestilling" section

### Why the Change?
- Improved user experience
- Better integration with Akershus systems
- Modernized technology

## Common Questions About Skoleskyss

### Q: What transport does skoleskyss cover?
**A:** Public transit (buses, metro, trams, ferries) from home to school. Student typically uses regular Ruter services; transport pass loaded onto reisekort (smart card) or digital app.

### Q: How much does it cost?
**A:** Skoleskyss is **heavily subsidized** by the county. Student cost is typically very low or free, depending on family income and school type. Specific prices on application portal.

### Q: How long does approval take?
**A:** Varies; typically 2-4 weeks. Check with Akershus fylkeskommune for timeline.

### Q: Can I appeal a denied application?
**A:** Yes. Contact Akershus fylkeskommune directly (not Ruter). Link to appeal process on application portal.

### Q: What if I move during the school year?
**A:** Inform Akershus fylkeskommune. May need to reapply or update your address.

### Q: Do I need a physical reisekort or can I use the app?
**A:** Both options available. Check application confirmation for details.

## Limitations & Caveats

1. **Eligibility determined by county** — Ruter provides portal; Akershus fylkeskommune sets rules
2. **Oslo is separate** — Do not confuse with Akershus; Oslo kommune handles its own system
3. **Annual eligibility checks** — May need to reapply or re-verify each school year
4. **Application deadlines** — Usually set by county; check portal for dates
5. **Income-based** — Cost/eligibility may depend on family income; income verification may be required
