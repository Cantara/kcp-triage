# Skill: Understand Ruter Tickets & Fares

## Overview
Ruter.no provides comprehensive information about ticket types, prices, and purchase channels. This skill covers navigating ticket information, understanding fare zones, and identifying where to buy.

## Accessing Ticket Information

### Method 1: From Navigation
1. Click **"Om våre billetter"** (About our tickets) in the top navigation bar
2. You are taken to https://ruter.no/om-vare-billetter

### Method 2: From Homepage
1. Visit https://ruter.no/
2. Look for the **"Sånn kjøper du billett"** (How to buy tickets) section
3. Click **"Se billetter"** or **"Her kjøper du billett"**

### Method 3: Direct Access
1. Visit https://ruter.no/om-vare-billetter
2. Or specific page: https://ruter.no/om-vare-billetter/her-kjoper-du-billett

## Ticket Types

### 1. Enkeltbillett (Single Trip Ticket)

**Valid for:**
- ONE journey (one trip from start to destination)
- **60 minutes** with **unlimited transfers** within the 60-minute window
- Travel within one zone (or across zones if purchased for multi-zone)

**Price ranges:**
- Depends on zones traveled (Oslo is roughly divided into 2-3 zones)
- Approximate: NOK 30-50 for typical Oslo journeys (prices vary)
- Child/youth discounts available

**When to use:**
- Occasional travelers
- Single trip without planning a longer pass
- Tourist on limited budget

**Purchase channels:**
- Ruter app (digital ticket, no physical card needed)
- On-board vehicle (bus, tram, ferry) — cashless payment preferred
- Physical reisekort (smart card) at retailers

---

### 2. Periodebillett (Period Pass)

Multiple duration options; choose one:

**24-hour pass**
- Valid for 24 hours from activation
- Unlimited journeys across all zones
- Price: ~NOK 100-150 (approximate)

**7-day pass**
- Valid for 7 consecutive calendar days
- Unlimited journeys across all zones
- Price: ~NOK 300-400 (approximate)

**30-day pass**
- Valid for 30 consecutive calendar days
- Unlimited journeys across all zones
- Price: ~NOK 900-1100 (approximate)
- Common for commuters

**365-day pass (annual pass)**
- Valid for full calendar year
- Unlimited journeys across all zones
- Price: ~NOK 8000-10000 (approximate)
- Best value for frequent year-round travelers

**Special: 90-day pass (new, testing phase)**
- Ruter is testing a new 90-day periodebillett
- Aimed at children and young people
- Check news section for details: https://ruter.no/nyheter/ny-periodebillett-for-barn-og-unge

**When to use:**
- Commuters (30-day)
- Frequent travelers (7-day)
- Tourists (24-hour)
- Year-round daily travelers (365-day)

**Purchase:**
- Ruter app (digital pass loaded to account)
- Physical reisekort (smart card) at retailers
- Some period passes auto-renew on reisekort

---

### 3. Turistbillett (Tourist Ticket)

**Oslo Pass**
- **Not a transport ticket** — but **includes free/unlimited bus, metro, tram, boat** travel in Oslo/Akershus
- Also includes museum entry discounts
- Valid for 24, 48, or 72 hours
- Price: ~NOK 350-600 depending on duration
- Purchase: https://www.visitoslo.com/no/aktiviteter-og-attraksjoner/oslo-pass/

**Rutertourist Kart (Tourist Map)**
- Downloadable/printable map showing tourist attractions and public transit routes
- **No fare value** — for planning only
- Download: https://ruter.no/om-vare-billetter/turistbillett

**When to use:**
- Tourists staying 1-3 days
- Plan to visit multiple museums + use transit extensively

## Fare Zones

### Zone System
Oslo and Akershus are divided into **zones** (typically 1-3 for main coverage):
- **Zone 1:** Central Oslo
- **Zone 2:** Greater Oslo
- **Zone 3:** Outer Akershus and suburbs

### How Zones Affect Fare
- **Single ticket:** Price depends on zones crossed
  - Zone 1 only: cheaper
  - Zone 1→2: more expensive
  - Zone 2→3: highest
- **Period passes:** All zones included (no surcharge)

### Checking Zones
- When you search in journey planner (reise.ruter.no), zones are indicated
- Ticket machine and app show zone(s) for your journey

## Purchasing Tickets

### Digital Ticket in Ruter App
1. Download app from: https://ruter.no/planlegg-reise/ruter-appen
2. iOS: Apple App Store | Android: Google Play Store
3. In app:
   - Create account or log in
   - Select ticket type (single or period)
   - Choose duration/zones
   - Pay with card
4. Ticket appears in app as QR code or digital pass
5. Show to conductor or scan at entry validators

**Advantages:**
- No physical card needed
- Instant purchase
- Auto-renewal options for period passes
- Discounts sometimes available

---

### Physical Reisekort (Smart Card)
1. Buy blank reisekort at retail locations (7-Eleven, kiosks, train stations)
2. Load ticket onto card using:
   - **Ruter app** (recommended)
   - **Ticket machines** at major stops/stations
   - **Retailer terminals**
3. Touch card to **validator** (yellow machine) when boarding
4. Balance/validity shown on card or in app

**Advantages:**
- Shareable (can pass to someone else)
- Familiar for regular travelers
- Works offline
- No smartphone required

---

### On-Board Purchase
1. Board vehicle (bus, tram, metro, ferry)
2. Look for **"Kjøp billett"** (buy ticket) sign or **ticket machine onboard**
3. Pay with card (cash rarely accepted; ask driver)
4. Receive digital or paper ticket
5. Keep ticket for duration of journey (may be checked)

**Limitations:**
- Only available on buses and some trams
- Limited to single tickets
- May have surcharge vs. pre-purchase
- Not available on metro (T-bane)

---

### Retailer Locations
Buy physical reisekort and top-up at:
- **7-Eleven stores** (city-wide)
- **Narvesen kiosks** (convenience stores)
- **Oslo Central Station (Oslo S)** and other major stations
- **Ruter Service Centers** (limited locations)

## Special Discounts & Categories

### Children
- Typically **free or heavily discounted** until age 6-7
- Reduced fares until age ~18 (varies by period pass)
- Student discounts available (with valid student ID)

### Youth/Students
- Discounts on period passes (7-day, 30-day, 365-day)
- Must show valid student ID or youth ID

### Seniors
- Discounted fares for age 67+ (varies by ticket type)
- Many senior passes exist; check website for current offerings

### Accessibility
- Blind/visually impaired: often free or discounted
- Mobility-impaired passengers + 1 companion: may have reduced fares
- Contact Ruter for specific accessibility pricing

## Prices & Current Info

**Prices listed on ruter.no are estimates** — actual prices may vary.

For **current, accurate prices:**
1. Visit https://ruter.no/om-vare-billetter/enkeltbillett
2. Or: https://ruter.no/om-vare-billetter/periodebillett
3. Check the **"Se billettpriser"** (See ticket prices) link
4. Use the **Ruter app** which always shows current prices

## Travel Guarantee & Refunds

### Retur-Garantien (Travel Guarantee)
Ruter offers compensation if:
- Bus/metro/tram is **15+ minutes delayed**
- You purchase a return trip same day
- Compensating deduction from next ticket

More info: https://ruter.no/fa-hjelp-og-kontakt (search for "Retur-Garantien")

### Lost/Unused Tickets
- **Digital tickets:** Can be refunded via app (check policy)
- **Physical reisekort:** Non-refundable; card can be reused
- **Period passes:** Non-refundable once activated

## Tips for Choosing Tickets

1. **One or two trips?** → Buy single tickets (enkeltbillett)
2. **Visiting for 1-2 days?** → Buy 24-hour pass or Oslo Pass
3. **Daily commuter?** → Buy 30-day pass (best value)
4. **Yearly frequent traveler?** → Buy 365-day pass (significant savings)
5. **Tourist on tight budget?** → Check if Oslo Pass worth it (includes museums)
6. **Unsure of zones?** → Buy single ticket; system will charge correct zone automatically
7. **Prefer not to use app?** → Get physical reisekort and load via ticket machine

## Limitations & Caveats

1. **Prices change annually** — Ruter typically adjusts fares in January
2. **60-minute rule on singles** — Must complete journey within 60 minutes
3. **Zone system can be confusing** — Use journey planner to determine zones before buying
4. **No refunds on period passes** — Once activated, cannot be refunded
5. **App requires account creation** — Identity verification may be required
6. **Accessibility pricing varies** — Contact Ruter for specific categories and rates
