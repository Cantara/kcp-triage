Navigate English-language resources and international applicant-specific information for applying to Norwegian higher education.

## Important External Resources Linked from English Pages

### Study in Norway (Official Portal)
- **URL:** https://www.studyinnorway.no
- **Purpose:** Comprehensive information about studying and living in Norway
- **Content:**
  - Program search (additional to Samordna opptak search)
  - Scholarship database
  - Student visa information
  - Housing guidance
  - Testimonials from international students

### Graduate Secondary Unit (GSU) List
- **URL:** https://hkdir.no/en/foreign-education/lists-and-databases/higher-education-entrance-qualification-gsu
- **Purpose:** Check if your foreign qualification is recognized as equivalent to Norwegian upper secondary education
- **How to Use:**
  1. Go to the GSU list
  2. Search by your country and qualification type
  3. Verify your qualification appears on the list
  4. If NOT listed, you may not meet minimum admission requirements
  5. Contact institution directly if unclear

### Admission Portal
- **URL:** https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/
- **Purpose:** Search programs and submit applications
- **Study Overview Links:**
  - Universities/Colleges: https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/admission/18/studies
  - Vocational Schools: https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/admission/19/studies
- **Note:** Portal is available in English once logged in

## Step-by-Step: International Applicant Journey

### Step 1: Check Your Qualification
1. Go to https://hkdir.no/en/foreign-education/lists-and-databases/higher-education-entrance-qualification-gsu
2. Search for your country and qualification type
3. Verify it appears on the GSU list
4. **If not listed:** You may not be eligible; contact Samordna opptak or the institution directly

### Step 2: Verify Language Requirements
1. Navigate to `/english/language-requirements.html`
2. Check if your program is taught in English
3. If English-taught: Note required English proficiency level and tests accepted
4. If Norwegian-taught: You need Norwegian language proficiency
5. Prepare language test if needed (TOEFL, IELTS, etc.)

### Step 3: Understand General Requirements
1. Go to `/english/general-requirements.html`
2. Read about admission criteria
3. Understand point calculation system (if applicable)
4. Note any program-specific prerequisites

### Step 4: Search for Programs
1. Go to https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/admission/18/studies (uni/college)
2. Or https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/admission/19/studies (vocational)
3. Filter by:
   - **Language:** English
   - **Institution:** Your preferred location/university
   - **Field:** Your area of interest
4. Review point borders (poenggrenser) for competitiveness
5. Make a list of target programs

### Step 5: Check Deadlines
1. Return to `/english/` main page
2. Note key deadlines:
   - 15 April (main)
   - 1 July (supplementary)
   - 20 July (waiting list)
3. Add to your calendar

### Step 6: Check Tuition & Costs
1. Click link to https://studyinnorway.no/tuition-fees-students
2. Verify tuition fee status for your country of residence
3. Check whether you're subject to tuition (non-EU/EEA) or not (EU/EEA)
4. Review cost of living and scholarships

### Step 7: Prepare Documentation
1. **Language certificate:** TOEFL/IELTS if required
2. **Transcript:** Official grades from upper secondary school (translated to English)
3. **Diploma/Certificate:** Official copy of upper secondary diploma
4. **Motivation letter or CV:** If specific program requires
5. Have all documents ready to upload after applying

### Step 8: Apply via Portal
1. Go to https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/
2. Create account (registration in English)
3. Fill out application form
4. Select your programs and rank them by preference
5. Note: Electronic transcript system may differ for international applicants; check portal instructions
6. Submit before deadline (15 April for main round)

### Step 9: Upload Required Documents
1. After submission, return to portal
2. Check your application for required documentation
3. Upload:
   - English language test results (if required)
   - Official transcripts (translated)
   - Diploma copy (translated)
   - Any program-specific documents
4. **Deadline:** Typically 1 week after application deadline

### Step 10: Check Decision
1. Portal will notify you of decision (~15 May for main round)
2. Decision will specify:
   - **Offer (Tilbud):** Admitted
   - **Venteliste:** Waiting list
   - **Ikke tatt opp:** Not admitted
3. If admitted: Accept within deadline (usually 1 week)
4. If waitlisted: You may be offered place later if space opens

### Step 11: Enroll After Acceptance
1. Accept admission in portal
2. Contact institution directly for:
   - Housing application
   - Course registration
   - International student services
   - Student visa requirements (if applicable)
3. Verify insurance and residence requirements

## Tips for International Applicants

1. **Start early:** Check GSU list and language requirements 6 months before deadline
2. **Prepare language tests:** TOEFL/IELTS takes time; plan ahead
3. **Translate documents:** Have official transcripts and diplomas translated to English or Norwegian
4. **Ask questions:** Contact institutions directly if information is unclear
5. **Use Study in Norway:** Excellent resource for non-admission questions (visa, housing, costs)
6. **Join student groups:** Many institutions have international student organizations
7. **Budget:** Factor in tuition (if non-EU/EEA) and living costs before applying
8. **Have a backup:** Apply to multiple programs; competition can be fierce

## Frequently Asked Questions for International Applicants

### Is my qualification recognized?
- **Answer:** Check the GSU list at https://hkdir.no/en/foreign-education/lists-and-databases/higher-education-entrance-qualification-gsu
- If not listed, contact the institution or Samordna opptak

### Do I need to speak Norwegian?
- **Answer:** Only if your program is taught in Norwegian
- Many English-taught programs available
- Check language requirement at `/english/language-requirements.html`

### What language test do I need?
- **Answer:** Depends on your program and native language
- Details at `/english/language-requirements.html`
- Usually TOEFL or IELTS if English is not your native language

### Will I pay tuition?
- **Answer:** Depends on your country of residence
- EU/EEA citizens: Usually free or low tuition
- Non-EU/EEA: Usually pay tuition
- Check https://studyinnorway.no/tuition-fees-students

### How do I apply?
- **Answer:** Via https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/
- Deadline: 15 April (main round)
- Create account → Fill form → Rank programs → Submit
- Upload documents within 1 week

### What happens after I apply?
- **Answer:** See `/jeg-har-sokt-hva-skjer-na.html` (in English when translated) or ask institution directly
- Timeline: April–May for processing, decision ~15 May
- Check portal for decision and upload documents if required
