# Skill: Understand the Application Process

## Overview
Navigate the step-by-step application workflow and understand what happens after submission, including documentation requirements, processing timelines, and decision notifications.

## Before You Apply: Preparation

### 1. Explore Programs
- Navigate to `/aktuelt/forberedelser-soke-samordna-opptak.html`
- Choose your pathway:
  - **Universities/University Colleges:** `/universitet-og-hogskole/`
  - **Vocational Schools:** `/fagskole/`
- Use embedded study search:
  - Universities/Colleges: https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/admission/18/studies
  - Vocational Schools: https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/admission/19/studies

### 2. Check Admission Requirements
- Go to `/universitet-og-hogskole/opptakskrav/` (universities/colleges)
- Or `/fagskole/opptakskrav/` (vocational schools)
- Verify you meet minimum qualifications
- For international applicants: see `/english/general-requirements.html`

### 3. Review Deadlines
- Universities/Colleges: `/universitet-og-hogskole/tidsfrister`
- Vocational Schools: `/fagskole/tidsfrister`
- Key dates:
  - 15 April (main deadline for universities/colleges)
  - 1 July (supplementary applications)
  - 20 July (waiting list offers)
  - Vocational school deadlines may differ

### 4. Understand Point Calculation
- See `/universitet-og-hogskole/poengberegning/` (uni/college)
- Or `/fagskole/poengberegning/` (vocational school)
- Check point borders (poenggrenser) for specific programs:
  - `/universitet-og-hogskole/poengberegning/poenggrenser.html`

### 5. Watch Information Videos
- Go to `/filmer/`
- Topics covered:
  - "Slik søker du" (How to Apply)
  - "Hvordan prioritere studieønskene" (How to Prioritize Your Choices)
  - "Laste opp dokumentasjon" (Upload Documentation)
  - "Jeg har søkt, hva skjer nå?" (After Application)

## When You Apply: The Application Process

### 1. Register with the Application Portal
- **Portal URL:** https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/
- **Access:** "Til søkerportalen" button available on every page
- **Steps:**
  - Create account with email and password
  - Verify email
  - Log in to portal

### 2. Fill Out Application
- In the portal, complete your application form
- Information typically required:
  - Personal details
  - Educational background
  - Study program choices (prioritized list)
  - Program-specific answers/essays (if required)
  - Contact information

### 3. Prioritize Your Study Choices
- Rank your preferred programs in order of priority
- Portal allows multiple choices (usually 5-10 depending on program type)
- Video guide available: `/filmer/` → "Hvordan prioritere studieønskene"
- Important: Only first-choice programs are considered in main round; lower choices considered in supplementary rounds if needed

### 4. Verify Electronic Transcript
- Your grades from upper secondary education are automatically submitted
- Check that your electronic vitnemål (transcript) appears in the portal
- If missing or incorrect, contact your school or support (see `/universitet-og-hogskole/slik-soker-du/elektronisk-vitnemal.html`)
- Electronic transcript is crucial for point calculation

### 5. Submit Application
- Review all information
- **Important:** Submit before the deadline (15 April for universities/colleges; varies for vocational schools)
- Portal will issue a receipt/confirmation (kvittering)
- Keep the confirmation number for reference

## After You Apply: What Happens Next

### 1. Check Your Receipt
- After submission, save or print your confirmation (kvittering)
- Contains:
  - Application number
  - Date submitted
  - List of programs applied to
- Available for download from the portal

### 2. Document Upload Requirements
- Some programs require additional documentation beyond electronic transcript
- Check your portal for required documents
- Examples may include:
  - English language certificates (TOEFL, IELTS)
  - Curriculum vitae or motivation letter
  - Portfolio (for creative programs)
  - Medical/health documentation
- **Deadline:** Typically 1 week after application deadline
- **How to upload:** See video guide `/filmer/` → "Laste opp dokumentasjon"
- **Process:** Log into portal → Upload section → Select documents → Submit

### 3. Application Processing Timeline
- Samordna opptak and individual institutions review all applications
- Timeline (as of 2026):
  - **Main application deadline:** 15 April
  - **Document deadline:** ~22 April
  - **Processing period:** April–May
  - **Decision date (main round):** ~15 May
  - Notifications sent via:
    - Portal message
    - Email
    - SMS (if provided)

### 4. Supplementary Admissions (if applicable)
- If not admitted in main round, may be considered for supplementary round
- **Supplementary deadline:** 1 July
- **Decision:** ~20 July
- Portal will indicate if you are eligible for supplementary round

### 5. Understand Your Decision
- **Offer (Tilbud):** You have been admitted. Deadline to accept is typically 1 week.
- **Waitlist (Venteliste):** You are on a waiting list; may be admitted if space opens.
- **Not Admitted (Ikke tatt opp):** Your application did not meet the cut-off points or requirements.
- **Portal:** Check detailed decision letter in portal for reason and next steps

### 6. Accept or Decline Your Offer
- If admitted, you MUST respond in the portal by the deadline (usually ~1 week)
- **Accept:** Confirm you will enroll
- **Decline:** Decline the place (opens it for waiting list)
- **Failure to respond:** May result in forfeiture of the place
- Platform will send email reminders

### 7. Enroll After Acceptance
- After accepting your offer, follow enrollment instructions from the institution
- May include:
  - Housing applications
  - Course registration
  - Orientation attendance
  - Final documentation (passport, health insurance for international students)
- Contact the specific institution for enrollment details

## Important Notes

1. **Deadlines are strict:** Late applications are typically rejected; submit well before the deadline.
2. **Point calculation is automatic:** Your electronic transcript is used to calculate your points. Incorrect transcript = incorrect point calculation.
3. **Document upload is separate:** Even if your application is submitted, you must still upload required documents by the specified deadline.
4. **Applicant portal is separate:** This main site (www.samordnaopptak.no) is informational only. All actual interactions (submit, check status, upload documents) happen at https://sok.samordnaopptak.no/#/.
5. **Contact support if needed:** Questions about your specific application should go to Samordna opptak via `/om/kontakt/`.

## Troubleshooting

### Missing Electronic Transcript
- See `/universitet-og-hogskole/slik-soker-du/elektronisk-vitnemal.html`
- Contact your upper secondary school to verify transcript submission

### Can't Find Required Document Information
- Check portal directly (specific documents listed in your portal)
- Email the institution directly (contact info on institution's website)
- Call Samordna opptak support (number on `/om/kontakt/`)

### Decision Unclear
- Read the full decision letter in the portal
- Email the admissions office of the institution
- Call Samordna opptak for general questions

### Missed Deadline
- Some deadlines cannot be extended (strict policy)
- Contact the institution immediately to ask if late submission is possible
- Consider applying in supplementary round (1 July) if eligible
