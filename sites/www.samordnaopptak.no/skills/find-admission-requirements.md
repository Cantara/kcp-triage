# Skill: Find Admission Requirements

## Overview
Locate and interpret admission rules, prerequisites, qualifications, and language requirements for Norwegian higher education programs.

## For Universities & University Colleges

### General Requirements
1. Navigate to `/universitet-og-hogskole/opptakskrav/`
2. Page covers:
   - General admission criteria for Norwegian applicants
   - Point calculation system (explained separately)
   - Admission quotas and special categories
   - Norwegian language requirements for courses taught in Norwegian
   - Requirements for students with education from outside Norway (GSU list reference)

### International Applicants
1. Go to `/english/` → "General requirements" or "Language requirements"
2. Contains:
   - English-taught program requirements
   - Language proficiency standards (e.g., TOEFL, IELTS levels if applicable)
   - Recognition of foreign credentials
   - Reference to GSU (Graduate Secondary Unit) list for qualification equivalency
   - Link to external resources: https://hkdir.no/en/foreign-education/lists-and-databases/higher-education-entrance-qualification-gsu

### Point Calculation & Grades
1. Navigate to `/universitet-og-hogskole/poengberegning/`
2. Explains how points are calculated from grades
3. See point borders by program: `/universitet-og-hogskole/poengberegning/poenggrenser.html`
   - Shows cut-off points for admission in main round and supplementary round
   - Filters by institution and program

### Quotas & Special Admissions
1. Go to `/universitet-og-hogskole/opptakskvoter/`
2. Lists:
   - General capacity of each program
   - Special admission categories (e.g., people with disabilities, second-chance students)
   - Regional distribution if applicable

### Electronic Transcript Requirements
1. Navigate to `/universitet-og-hogskole/slik-soker-du/elektronisk-vitnemal.html`
2. Explains:
   - How electronic transcripts (vitnemål) are submitted automatically
   - What students need to do themselves
   - How transcripts relate to point calculation
   - Who to contact if transcript is missing

## For Vocational Schools (Fagskole)

### General Requirements
1. Navigate to `/fagskole/opptakskrav/`
2. Similar to university requirements but specific to vocational programs
3. May have different point thresholds or prerequisites

### Point Calculation
1. Go to `/fagskole/poengberegning/`
2. Explains vocational school-specific point system
3. May differ from university point rules

## For International Applicants (All Programs)

### Dedicated English Section
1. Navigate to `/english/`
2. Covers:
   - **General requirements:** Minimum qualifications accepted
   - **Language requirements:** English proficiency for English-taught programs
   - **Tuition fees:** External link to https://studyinnorway.no/tuition-fees-students (EU/EEA vs. non-EU/EEA)
   - **Postgraduate programs:** Info for master's level study
   - **GSU list reference:** For credential equivalency

### Contact for Specific Programs
1. If requirements are unclear, go to `/om/kontakt/`
2. Find contact info for:
   - Samordna opptak (central admissions body)
   - Individual institutions
   - Vocational schools (list: `/om/kontakt/liste-fagskoler.html`)

## Key Definitions

### Poenggrenser (Point Borders)
- Minimum score needed to be admitted to a program
- Varies by program, institution, and admission round
- See `/universitet-og-hogskole/poengberegning/poenggrenser.html`

### Elektronisk Vitnemål (Electronic Transcript)
- Automatic submission of grades from upper secondary school
- Reviewed as part of admission decision
- See page: `/universitet-og-hogskole/slik-soker-du/elektronisk-vitnemal.html`

### Opptakskvoter (Admission Quotas)
- Number of places available in each program
- May include reserved spots for special groups
- See `/universitet-og-hogskole/opptakskvoter/`

### GSU List
- Recognition list for foreign upper secondary qualifications
- Link: https://hkdir.no/en/foreign-education/lists-and-databases/higher-education-entrance-qualification-gsu

## Search Tips
- Use site search for specific keywords: "opptakskrav", "poenggrenser", "vitnemål", "språkkrav"
- English content at `/english/` is specifically written for international applicants
- Each program may have unique requirements; check institution websites for details
- Contact info provided on `/om/kontakt/` if site doesn't answer the question
