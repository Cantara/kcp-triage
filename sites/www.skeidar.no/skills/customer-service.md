# Skill: Access Customer Service & Support

## Overview
This skill covers how to find help, contact Skeidar, access policies, and resolve common issues.

## Main Customer Service Hub
**URL:** `https://www.skeidar.no/kundeservice/`

**What you'll find:**
Central hub with links to all support topics, organized by category.

## Contact Information

### Store Locator & Phone Numbers
**URL:** `/kundeservice/kontakt/`

**Features:**
- **Google Maps embedded** showing all Skeidar physical store locations across Norway
- **Click any store pin** to see:
  - Store name
  - Full address
  - Phone number (for in-store inquiries)
  - Store hours (opening/closing times)
  - Distance from current location (if location permission granted)

**How to use:**
1. Go to `/kundeservice/kontakt/`
2. Allow browser location permission (optional; shows nearest stores)
3. Click on a store pin
4. Note phone number for in-store questions
5. Call during business hours for immediate help

### Email Contact
- **Email:** netthandel@skeidar.no
- **For:** Online orders, website issues, returns, billing questions, product inquiries
- **Response time:** Usually 1-2 business days

### When to Contact Each

| Question Type | Best Contact | Why |

6. Await refund confirmation

### 6. Financing Options
**URL:** `/kundeservice/finansiering/`

**Available financing:**
- **0% APR installments:**
  - 6 months
  - 12 months
  - 24 months
  - (Subject to approval & income verification)
- **Financing partners:**
  - Klarna (buy now, pay later)
  - Santander (installment loans)

**How to apply:**
1. Add items to cart (usually triggered for orders over certain threshold, e.g., 3,000-5,000 NOK)

8. Complete order with financed amount

**Typical eligibility:**
- Must be Norwegian resident
- 18+ years old
- Valid bank account
- Regular income
- Good credit score (varies by lender)

## Special Guarantees & Programs

### Price Guarantee (Prisløfte)
**URL:** `/kundeservice/prislofte/`

**Promise:**
Skeidar guarantees the lowest market price on all furniture. If customer finds lower price elsewhere, Skeidar will match or beat it.

**How to claim:**
1. Find same product at competitor (same brand, model, condition)
2. Screenshot competitor's price & product page
3. Email to netthandel@skeidar.no with:
   - Your order number
   - Competitor name & URL
   - Screenshot of competitor price
4. Skeidar reviews claim (usually within 1-2 business days)
5. If valid, refund difference or match price
6. Refund issued to original payment method

**Exclusions:**
- Grey market sellers (unauthorized distributors)
- Liquidation sales or clearance events
- Damaged, refurbished, or open-box items
- Items from different region (e.g., Danish price vs. Norwegian)
- Discontinued models

### Comfort Guarantee – Mattress 100-Day Trial (Komfortgaranti)
**URL:** `/kundeservice/komfortgaranti/`

**Promise:**
Customer can try any mattress from Skeidar for 100 days. If not satisfied, return for full refund (no questions asked).

**How it works:**
1. Buy any mattress (all brands, models)
2. Deliver to home
3. Sleep on it for up to 100 days
4. If not comfortable or satisfied:
   - Contact customer service
   - Request return
   - Skeidar arranges pickup
   - Receive full refund (may deduct original shipping cost)
5. Process typically takes 5-10 business days

**Why this matters:**
- Mattress comfort is subjective; 100 days is enough to know
- No risk to customer; try before committing
- Applies to all mattress types: spring, memory foam, latex, hybrid

## Contacting Customer Service – Best Practices

### Before You Contact
1. **Check product page:** Most specs/options listed
2. **Check customer service hub:** `/kundeservice/` may answer your question
3. **Check confirmation email:** Tracking & order details in email
4. **Check payment/invoice:** Details in confirmation email

### How to Contact

**Email (recommended for complex issues):**
- Address: netthandel@skeidar.no
- Subject line: "[ORDER #] – Brief description" (e.g., "[12345678] – Delivery damage claim")
- Include:
  - Order number
  - Product name
  - Clear description of issue
  - Photos (if relevant)
  - Preferred resolution
- Response time: 1-2 business days

**Phone (fastest for quick questions):**
- Find local store: `/kundeservice/kontakt/`
- Call during store hours
- Have order number ready
- Be specific about issue

**In-store (immediate help):**
- Visit nearest Skeidar location
- Bring order confirmation (email or print)
- Speak to staff in-person
