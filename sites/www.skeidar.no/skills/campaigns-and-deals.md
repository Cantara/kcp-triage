# Skill: Find Sales, Campaigns & Deals on Skeidar

## Overview
Skeidar runs several ongoing campaigns and sales. This skill teaches how to navigate to deals, understand the types of offers, and find the best prices.

## Where to Find Campaigns

### Main Campaign Hub
**URL:** `https://www.skeidar.no/ukens-kampanjer/`

**What you'll see:**
- Overview of all active weekly campaigns
- Featured campaign banners
- Quick links to popular deals

### Campaign Pages

#### 1. "Alltid Lav Pris" (Always Low Price)
**URL:** `https://www.skeidar.no/ukens-kampanjer/alltid-lav-pris/`

**What it is:**
- Permanent price-matching guarantee campaign
- Skeidar guarantees they have the market's lowest price on all furniture
- Not a traditional "sale" but a promise of competitive pricing

**Key messaging:**
- "Garantert markedets laveste pris" (Guaranteed lowest market price)
- If customer finds lower price elsewhere, Skeidar will match it
- No conditions or fine print—straightforward promise

**How to use:**
- Browse all products knowing prices are guaranteed competitive
- Link to price guarantee policy: `/kundeservice/prislofte/`

#### 2. "Beste Kupp" (Best Deals)
**URL:** `https://www.skeidar.no/ukens-kampanjer/beste-kupp/`

**What it is:**
- Hand-picked selection of items on sale or heavily discounted
- Featured prominently on homepage and campaign hub
- Items change weekly/seasonally

**What you'll see:**
- Product grid with sale badges
- Original price crossed out, new sale price shown
- Discount percentage (e.g., "30% rabatt" / "30% off")
- Stock status indicator

**How to navigate:**
- Click any product to view details
- See if you can add to cart (check stock status)
- Sale prices apply automatically at checkout

#### 3. Weekly Campaigns (Seasonal)
**URL:** `https://www.skeidar.no/ukens-kampanjer/`

**Examples of recurring campaigns:**
- Spring/summer collections

## Finding Discounted Products

### Method 1: Use Sale Filter
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to `/alle-produkter/`
2. In left sidebar, find "Onsale" or "Sale status" filter
3. Check box: "Onsale" or similar
4. Products update to show only discounted items

**URL example:**
```
https://www.skeidar.no/alle-produkter/?filter=onsale|sale&sort=6&page=1
```

### Method 2: Browse by Brand Sale
**URLs with multiple brands on sale:**
```
https://www.skeidar.no/alle-produkter/?filter=p_brand|Brunstad;p_brand|Conform;p_brand|Jensen;onsale|sale&sort=6&page=1
```

**Top brands often on sale:**
- Brunstad
- Conform
- Formfin
- Furninova
- Hjellegjerde
- Jensen
- Stressless
- Svane
- Wonderland
- XOV

### Method 3: Stock-Based Fast Delivery
**Filter:** "Rask levering" (Fast delivery) or "Webshop" stock

**URL:**
```
https://www.skeidar.no/alle-produkter/?filter=stock_status|REGIONALWAREHOUSE;stock_status|WEBSHOP&sort=6&page=1
```

**Why it's a deal:**
- Items in stock = guaranteed quick delivery (2-5 business days)
- Often items with better pricing to move inventory
- Same warranty/guarantee applies

## Understanding Sale Prices

### How to Read a Sale
**Product card shows:**
```
Original price:  [Crossed out]
Sale price:      [Highlighted in red/bold]
Discount:        "30% rabatt" or "-1,000 kr"
```

**Example:**
```
Sofa Stressless: 15,000 kr → 10,500 kr (30% off)
```

### Sale Price Guarantees
- **Prisløfte applies:** If price drops further after purchase, customer can request price match
- Policy: `/kundeservice/prislofte/`

### When Prices Update
- Prices update automatically in cart at checkout
- No code needed (unless special promo code campaign)
- Sale prices honored at time of order confirmation

## Promo Codes & Special Offers

### Where to Find Codes
1. **Email newsletter** – Sign up at homepage footer
2. **Weekly campaigns** – Often include exclusive codes
3. **Social media** – Instagram: `@skeidar_no` or `insta-skeidar`
4. **Physical stores** – In-store only promotions (ask customer service)

### How to Apply a Promo Code
1. Add items to cart
2. Go to `/cart/` (or click cart icon)
3. Look for "Rabattkode" (Promo code) input box
4. Enter code (copy-paste to avoid typos)
5. Click "Bruk" (Apply) or "Legg til" (Add)
6. Discount applies to total
7. Proceed to checkout

### Common Code Types
- **Percentage off:** e.g., "SAVE15" = 15% off entire order
- **Fixed amount:** e.g., "SAVE500" = 500 kr off
- **Free shipping:** e.g., "SHIPFREE" = No shipping cost
- **Category specific:** e.g., "SOFASALE" = Sofas only
- **First-time customer:** e.g., "WELCOME" = Discount on first order

**Note:** Codes may have conditions (minimum order, exclusions, expiration date).

## Special Guarantees & Offers

### Mattress Comfort Guarantee (Komfortgaranti)
**Link:** `/kundeservice/komfortgaranti/`

**What it is:**
- 100-day trial on all mattresses
- Customer can return mattress for full refund within 100 days
- No questions asked
- Applies to all mattress brands and models

**How to use:**
- Buy any mattress
- Try it at home for up to 100 days
- If not satisfied, contact customer service to arrange return
- Receive full refund (minus any shipping costs for return)

### Price Guarantee (Prisløfte)
**Link:** `/kundeservice/prislofte/`

**What it is:**
- Skeidar guarantees lowest price on market
- If customer finds same product cheaper elsewhere, Skeidar will:
  - Match the price, OR
  - Discount further to beat competitor

**How to claim:**
- Find competitor offering lower price (same brand/model)
- Contact customer service: netthandel@skeidar.no
- Provide proof (screenshot, competitor URL)
- Skeidar adjusts price or issues refund for difference
- Must be claimed before order ships

**Exclusions:**
- Grey market/unauthorized sellers
- Liquidation sales
- Damaged/refurbished items

### Free Shipping Threshold
**Offer:** Free shipping on orders over 5,000 NOK

**How it works:**
- Subtotal must reach 5,000 NOK to qualify
- Automatically applied at checkout
- Applies to standard delivery (not express)
- Display on checkout: "Gratis frakt over 5000 kr"

## Financing Options

**Available options:**
- **0% APR installments:** 6, 12, or 24 months (subject to approval)
- **Partners:** Klarna, Santander, and other Norwegian fintech lenders
- **Eligibility:** Automatic at checkout for high-value orders

## Comparison Shopping Tips

### Quick Price Check
1. **Know the model number:** e.g., "Stressless Recliner Model 1213"
2. **Check Skeidar price** on product page
3. **Search competitor:** Use Google to find competitor prices
4. **If lower found:** Use Skeidar's price guarantee to match or beat

### Basket Size & Shipping Impact
- **Under 5,000 NOK:** Add shipping cost (~300-500 kr) to comparison
- **Over 5,000 NOK:** Skeidar offers free shipping (advantage)
- **Total cost comparison:** Always include shipping in final price

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Sale price not showing in cart?**
- Refresh page
- Clear browser cache
- Logout and log back in (if applicable)

**Q: Promo code not working?**
- Verify code is typed correctly (may be case-sensitive)
- Check expiration date (codes expire)
- Confirm code applies to items in cart (some exclude sale items)
- Contact customer service if code should work: netthandel@skeidar.no

**Q: Product out of stock at sale price?**
- Check regional warehouse option ("Rask levering")
- Set email alert ("Meld meg på når varen er tilbake" / "Notify me when back in stock")
- Browse similar products in campaign
